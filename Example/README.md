### More Drama

This example app shows some best pratices for a simple Swift app, using **Core Data** + **More Data**!

**More Drama** is a thrilling mystery about corporate espionage... Several characters share their gossip around one particular night, where a prototype AI device -- usually locked away in a high-security safe -- was stolen! The hunt for that _TeaBot-3000_, and AI device which predicts spicy gossip before it happens, will lead you to search through the noise to solve the mystery... who stole the prototype?!

This app is a demonstration of simplicity: once the data model is set up, the whole app is less than 100 lines of code!
